ANSWER (11.6s):
**Recovery when locked out of MFA** — if you've lost your MFA device and can't access email [source: it_password_reset.pdf, page 2]:

- From a different device, go to `https://help.acme.example/recovery`
- Click **Account recovery — no MFA access**
- Enter your Acme email and employee ID (found on your badge)
- Answer the three security questions you set during onboarding
- If successful, a one-time recovery link is sent to your backup email on file
- Then create a new password meeting the complexity rules (min. 14 characters, upper + lower + number + symbol, no reuse of last 10 passwords, no name/username, no dictionary words) [source: it_password_reset.pdf, page 2]

**If you also don't have your badge** (no access to any Acme system) [source: it_password_reset.pdf, page 3]:

- Contact your manager directly via personal phone
- Your manager opens a **P1 ticket** with IT Security on your behalf
- Visit an Acme office and present government-issued photo ID to an IT administrator for in-person verification
- IT then resets your account and schedules a new MFA enrollment session
- Typical turnaround: **4–24 hours**, depending on office availability

**Notes**
- IT will never ask for your password — don't share it with anyone [source: it_password_reset.pdf, page 3]
- If unresolved within 2 business days, escalate to your IT Business Partner (intranet → IT > Business Partners) [source: it_password_reset.pdf, page 3]
- Related: **IT-SOP-002: MFA Enrollment** covers re-enrolling your new device [source: it_password_reset.pdf, page 4]
