# Finance — Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Owner:** Finance Operations (`finance-ops@acme.example`)
**Applies to:** All full-time employees, contractors with approved SOWs.

## Scope

This policy governs reimbursement of business expenses incurred by employees
and approved contractors on behalf of Acme Corp. Expenses outside this
policy require prior written approval from a Director or above.

## Submission Deadlines

- Expenses must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the transaction
  date via Concur.
- Expenses older than 60 days will be rejected unless accompanied by a
  written exception approved by the Finance Director.
- Quarterly close: expenses for a given quarter must be submitted by the
  **5th business day** of the following quarter.

## Receipt Requirements

Receipts are required for:

- All expenses over **$25.00**
- All lodging expenses regardless of amount
- All international travel expenses regardless of amount

Receipts must show: vendor name, date, itemized charges, and payment method.
Credit card statements alone are NOT acceptable as receipts.

If a receipt is lost, submit a signed missing receipt affidavit (available
in Concur). More than three missing receipt affidavits in a rolling 12-month
period may trigger a review.

## Reimbursable Expenses

### Travel

- **Airfare**: Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy
  permitted for flights 6+ hours. Business class requires VP approval.
- **Lodging**: Up to $300/night in tier-1 US cities (NYC, SF, Boston,
  Chicago, DC, LA, Seattle); $200/night elsewhere domestic; international
  per GSA per diem.
- **Ground transport**: Uber/Lyft, taxis, rental cars (mid-size or smaller),
  public transit. Black car service requires VP approval.
- **Mileage**: Personal vehicle reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate.

### Meals

Per diem when traveling: **$75/day domestic, $100/day international**.
Actual meal costs within per diem are reimbursable with itemized receipts.
Alcohol is reimbursable for client meals only (not solo travel) up to 30%
of the meal total.

### Office and Supplies

- Home office equipment up to **$500/year** with manager pre-approval
- Software subscriptions require IT security review before purchase
- Books and training materials related to your role up to **$1,500/year**

### Conferences and Events

Requires manager pre-approval via a Conference Request form. Approved
conferences cover registration, travel, lodging, and meals per the above
guidelines.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are NEVER reimbursable:

- Traffic violations, parking tickets, and toll evasion fees
- Personal entertainment (movies, concerts, spa)
- Alcohol for solo travel or personal meals
- First-class airfare (economy-plus or premium-economy only)
- Spouse or family travel costs
- Lost or damaged personal items
- Hotel minibar, in-room movies, or gym fees
- Political contributions or lobbying expenses

## Corporate Card Policy

Corporate cards are issued to employees in roles that travel more than 4
times per year. The card must be used for business expenses only.
Personal use of the corporate card is a terminable offense.

Lost or stolen corporate cards must be reported within 24 hours by calling
the issuer's 24/7 line printed on the card.

## Approval Chain

- Under $500 — Manager approval
- $500 to $5,000 — Director approval
- Over $5,000 — VP approval
- Over $25,000 — CFO approval + Legal review

Expenses submitted without the correct approval level will be returned for
re-routing, extending the reimbursement timeline by 5–10 business days.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expenses are reimbursed via direct deposit on the next bi-weekly
payroll cycle (typically Fridays). Expected timeline from submission to
reimbursement:

1. Employee submits in Concur (Day 0)
2. Manager approval (1–3 business days)
3. Finance review (2–3 business days)
4. Payroll cycle (next Friday)

Total expected: **5–10 business days** for well-formed submissions.

## Audits

Finance conducts monthly audits on a randomized 5% sample of submitted
expenses and a 100% review on any expense over $10,000. Findings that
indicate fraud are escalated to HR and may trigger termination.
