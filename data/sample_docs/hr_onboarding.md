# HR Onboarding Guide — New Hire Playbook

This document walks new employees through their first two weeks at Acme Corp.
If any information conflicts with your offer letter, the offer letter takes
precedence. Contact `hr@acme.example` with questions.

## Day 1 — What to Expect

On your first day, you will:

1. Arrive at the main lobby by **9:00 AM**. Reception will issue your
   temporary badge — keep this on your person at all times.
2. Meet your onboarding buddy in the 4th floor lounge at 9:30 AM.
3. Attend the new-hire orientation session from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM in
   Conference Room Aurora.
4. Have lunch with your team lead (scheduled separately — check your calendar).
5. Complete IT setup with the helpdesk between 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM.

If you cannot make it in person on Day 1, notify your hiring manager and
`hr@acme.example` at least 48 hours in advance.

## Required Paperwork

All new hires must complete the following forms by the end of their first week:

- **Form W-4** (federal tax withholding)
- **Form I-9** (employment eligibility) — must be completed in person with
  original supporting documents
- **Direct deposit authorization** — bring a voided check or bank letter
- **Emergency contact form**
- **Acceptable Use Policy acknowledgement**
- **Confidentiality & IP Assignment Agreement**

Forms are available in Workday under `Personal > New Hire Paperwork`.
HR will follow up on any missing items on the Thursday of your first week.

## Benefits Enrollment Window

You have **30 calendar days** from your start date to enroll in benefits.
Outside that window, you must wait for the next open enrollment period
(November each year) or a qualifying life event.

Coverage starts on the **first of the month following your start date**.
Example: start date February 12 → coverage begins March 1.

Plans offered include medical (three tiers), dental, vision, FSA/HSA,
401(k) with 4% match vested immediately, life insurance, and short/long-term
disability.

## IT Access and Equipment

Your manager submits an equipment request via the ServiceNow IT portal no
later than 5 business days before your start date. Standard issue includes:

- Laptop (MacBook Pro 14" by default; Dell XPS 15 on request)
- One external monitor (up to two with manager approval)
- Keyboard, mouse, headset, laptop stand
- Badge access to your assigned floor and any team-specific labs

If your equipment is not at your desk on Day 1, open a P2 ticket with the
helpdesk at `help.acme.example`. Do NOT begin work on a personal device.

## Training Requirements

All new hires must complete the following training within **30 days**:

| Training                          | Provider       | Time   |
|-----------------------------------|----------------|--------|
| Security Awareness                | KnowBe4        | 45 min |
| Data Privacy & GDPR               | OneTrust       | 30 min |
| Anti-Harassment                   | LinkedIn Learning | 60 min |
| Code of Conduct                   | Internal LMS   | 20 min |

Failure to complete required training within 30 days results in temporary
access suspension.

## Your Onboarding Buddy

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy — typically a peer who has
been at Acme for at least 12 months and is not in your direct reporting line.
Your buddy is your go-to for cultural questions, unwritten norms, and
"who do I ask about X" routing.

Buddies meet with new hires for at least **four 30-minute sessions** over
the first month. These are not performance reviews — nothing said to your
buddy is reported to your manager.

## 30/60/90 Day Check-ins

Your manager will schedule structured check-ins at:

- **30 days**: Confirm tooling, access, and clarity on role expectations.
- **60 days**: Review first deliverables and adjust goals if needed.
- **90 days**: Formal probationary review — documented in Workday.

The 90-day review is a two-way conversation. You will also be asked to
submit feedback on the onboarding process itself.
